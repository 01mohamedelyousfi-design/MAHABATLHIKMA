# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Moroccan second-year Baccalaureate students studying philosophy independently, often on a phone or laptop. They need focused, accessible activities that help them move from recalling a lesson to comparing positions, applying concepts, and producing a reasoned philosophical synthesis.

## Product Purpose

محبة الحكمة makes philosophy approachable through contemporary Arabic learning experiences. Its lesson flow should help students understand philosophical problems rather than memorize isolated answers. Success means a student can distinguish philosophers' positions, transfer those positions to unfamiliar situations, and write a structured synthesis in their own words.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, reflective, and encouraging. The voice should respect the learner's intelligence, explain mistakes without judgment, and make rigorous philosophical thinking feel achievable.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense textbook pages, exam-pressure interfaces, trick questions, punitive grading, childish gamification, decorative interactions that distract from learning, and automated claims about the correctness of open philosophical writing.

## Design Principles

1. Practice before summary: understanding is demonstrated through decisions and application, not passive rereading.
2. Explain every correction: feedback should identify the misconception and point students back to the relevant concept.
3. Scaffold without replacing thought: provide structure and prompts while preserving the student's own conclusion.
4. Make progress calm and visible: show completion without timers, streak pressure, or punitive scores.
5. Respect philosophical nuance: distinguish structural writing checks from judgments about philosophical correctness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support Arabic RTL layouts, mobile screens, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced motion preferences, and feedback that never relies on color alone. Activities should allow unlimited retries and should not impose time pressure.
